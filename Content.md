**10. Wellhead & Well Integrity Monitoring (NEW)**
Autonomous, real-time monitoring for remote and unmanned oil & gas wellheads — extending Million Technology's IoT and connectivity expertise into the energy sector, delivered through a LoRaWAN-based edge gateway platform (solution partner: MinFarm Tech / Hiberband-class technology).
- **Wireless annulus pressure monitoring** — Ex-certified (Zone 0/1) wireless pressure sensors transmit real-time A/B annulus and Xmas tree pressure readings
- **Autonomous, off-grid operation** — Solar and battery-powered gateway units suited to desert and offshore locations with no power or cellular coverage
- **Long-range wireless network** — LoRaWAN connectivity with up to 5 km range per gateway and capacity for up to 1,000 connected instruments
- **Satellite & 5G backhaul** — Secure data transmission back to HQ via satellite or 5G where terrestrial networks aren't available
- **Central monitoring dashboard** — Real-time well integrity data, leak-detection warnings, and anomaly alerts accessible remotely by the well integrity team
- **Hardened for hazardous environments** — ATEX/Ex-rated, IP66-enclosed electronics rated for wide temperature ranges
- **Clear operational value** — Reduces field intervention trips and OpEx, accelerates decision-making, and improves operational safety
